Questions people ask.

Which platforms do you manage?

Facebook and Instagram are the most common starting point. TikTok and other channels can be added when they make sense for the audience and content.

How often should a business post?

There is no universal number. The right cadence is one you can sustain with useful, on-brand content and clear goals.

Does social media help SEO?

Social activity is not a direct shortcut to Google rankings, but it can strengthen brand visibility, discovery, branded searches, content reach, and the overall trust people see when they research your business.
