Google Business Profile: what actually helps a local business get found?

Learn which Google Business Profile details matter for local visibility and customer trust in McAllen and the Rio Grande Valley.

Short answer: Complete, accurate information helps Google understand relevance and helps customers trust what they see. Categories, hours, services, photos, reviews, and the linked website should all tell the same story.

Choose the most accurate primary category

The primary category should describe the core business, not every possible service. Add relevant secondary categories only when they accurately represent what you do.

Keep information consistent

Your phone, website, hours, business name, and service details should match what appears on your website and major listings. Contradictory information creates friction for customers and weakens clarity.

Use photos that prove the business is real

Show the location when relevant, staff, work, products, vehicles, equipment, or the environment customers will actually see. Real visual evidence can do more for trust than generic stock photography.

Build a review process

Invite all customers to share honest feedback after a real experience, without incentives or screening for positive reviews. Respond to reviews professionally and consistently. Reviews are not only a rating; they are part of how a customer compares options.

Connect the profile to a useful website

If the website is slow, vague, or missing the service the customer searched for, the profile has done only half the job. The landing experience should continue the same message.

What should you do next?

Pick the biggest gap first. If customers cannot find you, start with local visibility. If they find you but do not trust or contact you, improve the website and proof. If people forget you between purchases, build a more consistent social presence.
