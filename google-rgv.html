How do I get my business to show up higher on Google in the RGV?

A practical guide for Rio Grande Valley businesses that want better Google and Maps visibility without gimmicks or keyword stuffing.

Short answer: Google local visibility is not one trick. Start with a complete Business Profile, accurate information, useful local pages, reviews, technical basics, and a site that makes sense to the customer after the click.

Start with Google Business Profile basics

Make sure your business name, primary category, phone number, website, hours, services, photos, and service areas are accurate. A profile that does not clearly describe the business makes relevance harder for both Google and customers.

Build pages around real services, not just cities

A useful page should answer what you do, who it is for, what the customer should expect, and where the service is available. A thin page that only swaps “McAllen” for “Mission” is not a useful local strategy.

Treat reviews as both trust and local prominence

Reviews can influence whether people choose you and are one of the signals Google mentions when discussing local prominence. The goal is not to manufacture reviews. Build a consistent process for asking real customers after a successful interaction.

Make the website technically easy to crawl

Use descriptive titles, one clear main heading, internal links, fast mobile pages, canonical URLs, a sitemap, crawlable text, and Search Console. These are boring compared with “SEO hacks,” but they are foundational.

Measure leads, not just rankings

Track calls, forms, bookings, direction requests, and the pages that create those actions. A keyword moving from position eight to five is useful only if the visibility produces better business outcomes.

What should you do next?

Pick the biggest gap first. If customers cannot find you, start with local visibility. If they find you but do not trust or contact you, improve the website and proof. If people forget you between purchases, build a more consistent social presence.
