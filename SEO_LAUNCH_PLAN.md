## Local ranking reality
“Marketing near me” should not be treated as a phrase to repeat. Local results depend heavily on relevance, distance, and prominence. The website supports relevance and prominence; the Google Business Profile, physical/service-area setup, reviews, citations, and links have to support it too.

## Content rules
- Do not create dozens of thin city pages with the same text.
- Do not promise #1 rankings.
- Do not fake reviews, case-study metrics, or client outcomes.
- Do not build “AI SEO” pages around gimmicks. Google says normal SEO fundamentals still apply to AI features.
- Add Spanish pages only when there is genuinely useful Spanish content, not machine-translated doorway pages.
